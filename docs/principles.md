# Product principles

Yadong set these on 2026-11-15 (T796). Weigh every design and trade-off against them first: when a change makes one
of them worse, do not make it, or find another way. Later decisions of his override earlier ones.

1. **Least effort to put things in.** Say it when it comes to mind; no thinking about where it goes or how to word it.
2. **Output that is the easiest to take in, in whatever form fits best.** What comes back is the easiest to
   understand and shown the way that suits it.
3. **Always moving.** Agents carry on without being pushed; when stuck they ask, they never just stop.
4. **Everything visible.** For each piece of work, how far it got and who it waits on is clear at a glance.
5. **His word wins.** What he decided is never changed quietly or lost; a later decision overrides an earlier one.
6. **Few interruptions.** What an agent can decide, it decides; it asks only what is really his to decide, all at once.

Also:

- **The phone and the desktop work alike:** the same thing sits in the same place, with the same name, on both.
- **The data stays his:** everything is on this machine, reached from outside only with the key; nothing is lost,
  and what was deleted can be brought back.
